# Sandooq El-Amara Building Management Skill

This skill allows agents to understand and assist users with residential building finance, unit tracking, maintenance dues, and expense reporting in Sandooq El-Amara.

## Capabilities

- **Building Dues Calculation**: Calculate monthly subscription fees, elevator maintenance, guard fees, and shared electricity costs.
- **Arrears Tracking**: Track unpaid monthly unit dues and generate payment receipts.
- **Financial Reports**: Export monthly and annual financial summaries for building residents.
