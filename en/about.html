About Sandooq El-Amara

Sandooq El-Amara is a specialized mobile app for managing accounts, monthly collections, and maintenance expenses for residential buildings and Homeowners Associations (HOA) in Egypt.

"Our core mission is to eliminate day-to-day disputes caused by handwritten paper ledgers and messy Excel sheets, offering an intuitive tool that grants building treasurers peace of mind and residents complete financial transparency for every single pound paid."

Why Did We Start This Project?

The concept emerged directly from everyday reality in Egyptian residential properties. Anyone stepping into the shoes of a building treasurer or joining an HOA board encounters familiar hurdles:

  • Lost paper receipts or forgetting who paid for which month.
  • The awkwardness of reminding neighbors about arrears without formal documentation.
  • Difficulty balancing the doorman's cash custody, plumbing supplies, and staircase lightbulb expenses.
  • Complex cost allocations for special projects like elevator overhauls or facade repainting.

That is why we engineered Sandooq El-Amara to be straightforward, mobile-first, and fully operational even without an active internet connection.

Our Core Values

🔒 Privacy First

Your property records and financial numbers belong solely to you; we never sell or share your data with advertisers or third parties.

⚡ Simplicity & Speed

A user-friendly design crafted for everyone, allowing you to log payments and dispatch WhatsApp receipts within seconds.

📱 Works 100% Offline

Record dues on the spot inside building lobbies or garages, with seamless encrypted cloud synchronization whenever network is restored.

📊 Complete Transparency

Export comprehensive PDF and Excel accounting reports ready for printing or instant sharing to the building's WhatsApp group.
