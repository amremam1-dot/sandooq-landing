Frequently Asked Questions About Subscriptions

How does instant activation work with InstaPay?
As soon as you transfer to our registered IPA address (amremam@instapay) and click "Transferred", our server links your verified account and immediately unlocks all features under a 48-hour trust grace period. Once our team reconciles the bank statement, your annual/monthly license is finalized permanently.
Can I pay via Google Play In-App Purchases instead?
Yes! You can subscribe directly through the Sandooq El-Amara Android app via standard Google Play billing using international credit/debit cards or Google Play balances.
What happens to my data if my subscription expires?
Your building records are never erased or held hostage. You can always access your historical reports and view stored apartments and expenses locally on your device. Only active cloud sync and automated WhatsApp features require an ongoing subscription.
