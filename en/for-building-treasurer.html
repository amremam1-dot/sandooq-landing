💼 The Treasurer's Role

Manage Property Accounts with Zero Awkwardness & No Late Nights on Paper Ledgers

You volunteer your personal time and energy to care for your community and maintain your building. You shouldn't have to face doubts, skepticism, or spend hours searching for misplaced receipts. Sandooq El-Amara was engineered to make your management fully transparent, verifiable, and stress-free.

Top 4 Challenges the App Solves for Treasurers:

1. Preventing Awkward Disputes Over Dues

Whenever a resident pays, send them an authenticated WhatsApp receipt card with one tap. It clearly itemizes the date, months covered, and outstanding balance—leaving zero room for claims like "I paid you and you forgot to log it."

2. Tracking Doorman Petty Cash & Minor Receipts

Disburse a cash float to the building custodian and log it as "Petty Cash." Every time he buys cleaning materials or plumbing supplies, log the bill—it deducts from his float automatically so you know his balance in real time.

3. Instant Arrears Statements for Assembly Meetings

Before any HOA general assembly meeting, you won't need hours to tally records; with a single tap, export an undeniable itemized arrears statement as a polished PDF or Excel file ready to present to owners.

4. Major Renovation Projects & Fund Advances

When the elevator needs an urgent overhaul, create an independent ledger for the project, set floor contributions with ground-floor exemptions, and use "Fund Advances" to cover temporary deficits while tracking repayments.

Transparency That Protects You and Satisfies Residents
