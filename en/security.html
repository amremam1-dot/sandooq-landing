The 5 Security Pillars of Sandooq El-Amara

We employ a Defense-in-Depth strategy across every touchpoint—from physical silicon hardware on your phone to cloud infrastructure:

1. Hardware-Backed Local Isolation (Device-Level Defense)

Device Security

Your local database is powered by Android Room/SQLite within the protected Android Application Sandbox. No other app installed on your smartphone can access or read your building files.

  • File-Based Encryption (FBE): Data benefits from modern AES-256 processor silicon encryption, with cryptographic keys anchored inside the hardware Android Keystore.
  • Biometric App Lock: Enable fingerprint or biometric facial authentication alongside custom PINs to block unauthorized access if your phone is borrowed or unlocked.
  • Offline-First Guarantee: Calculations, ledger balances, and receipts execute entirely on-device without mandatory continuous internet connection.

2. Data-in-Transit & Network Defense (TLS 1.3 Encryption)

Transit Encryption

When performing cloud synchronization or exporting data, all network requests are hardened according to modern cybersecurity mandates:

  • Strict TLS 1.3 Protocol: Traffic is secured using certificates from Google Trust Services, eliminating man-in-the-middle attacks.
  • Network Security Configuration: The Android app enforces certificate pinning policies, rejecting unauthorized proxy certificates.
  • Local Receipt Generation: WhatsApp receipts and PDF summaries are generated on-device and shared directly to the native WhatsApp app without intermediary servers.

3. Cloud Infrastructure & Identity (Google Cloud Platform)

Google Cloud

Cloud synchronization relies on Google Firebase and Google Cloud Platform, recognized among the most secure cloud architectures worldwide:

  • Google OAuth 2.0 Identity: Sign-ins are mediated by Google's secure OAuth flow with encrypted JWT session tokens—we never handle or store your email password.
  • Multi-Tenant Isolation via Firestore Security Rules: Declarative cloud rules verify caller identity and Building ID on every read and write, making cross-building data leakage impossible.
  • Global Data Center Certifications: Google Cloud data centers maintain compliance with ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 1/2/3, FedRAMP, and PCI-DSS.

4. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC Hierarchy)

Access Governance

5. Immutable Audit Trail & Disaster Recovery

Zero Data Loss

Robust accounting logic prevents retroactive falsification and ensures continuity across decades:

  • Immutable Financial Audit Trail: Each payment is stamped with an unforgeable timestamp, digital serial number, and operator identity. Ledger entries cannot be deleted silently without balancing audit entries.
  • 3-Tier Backup Strategy: 1) On-device SQLite database file (`.db`), 2) Encrypted cloud sync to your authorized Google account, 3) One-tap instant export to Excel spreadsheets and printable PDF reports.
  • Seamless Device Migration: Replacing a lost or broken smartphone restores your complete database in seconds upon signing in with your Google account.

Legal Governance & Regulatory Compliance

Sandooq El-Amara is built to align with rigorous statutory frameworks governing data privacy and property management:

⚖️ Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law (Law 151 of 2020)

We strictly enforce prior consent and process data exclusively for residential fund management, guaranteeing full user rights to review, export, or permanently erase records.

🏢 Egyptian Unified Building Law (Law 119 of 2008)

Our financial reporting formats comply with General Assembly auditing standards, ensuring statutory records and receipts are preserved for the mandatory 5-year inspection period.

🌍 General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR Principles)

We adhere to Data Minimization—never requesting national ID numbers or payment card data—and honor the Right to be Forgotten with one-click full data deletion.

Responsible Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

We collaborate actively with security researchers and white-hat developers to ensure our software remains impenetrable. If you identify a potential security vulnerability, please follow our disclosure policy:

  • Email our engineering security desk directly: security@sandoqalemara.com or amr@sandoqalemara.com with a detailed description and reproducible Proof-of-Concept (PoC).
  • Refrain from exploiting the vulnerability against live users or real building registries.
  • Allow our team reasonable time to remediate the issue prior to public disclosure.

SLA Commitment: Our security desk guarantees an initial response within 24 business hours, immediately initiating root-cause remediation and rolling out security patches. Valid submissions receive recognition on our Wall of Gratitude.
