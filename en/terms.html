1. Scope and Nature of Service

Sandooq El-Amara is a productivity software utility designed for building treasurers, property owners, and homeowner associations (HOAs / Occupants' Unions). It assists in logging maintenance dues, tracking arrears, registering expenses, managing petty cash, and generating formatted PDF statements and WhatsApp payment receipts.

The application is strictly an organizational accounting software tool. It does not provide banking, escrow, custodial, or money transmission services. Its function is limited to recording calculations and facilitating transparent communication between building residents and property administration.
