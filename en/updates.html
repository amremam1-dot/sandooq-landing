Version 1.0.221 (Latest) October 2026

Uncle Amin Smart Advisor, Interactive Voice Narrator, Multi-Month WhatsApp Receipts & Fluid Mobile Experience

A revolutionary milestone elevating Android usability with proactive financial guidance, natural voice walkthroughs, multi-month consolidated WhatsApp receipts, and fluid performance tailored for building treasurers and residents.

  • Uncle Amin Smart Advisor: Intelligent proactive banner inside the app analyzing treasury health in real time, alerting treasurers to critical collection dues, and suggesting proactive deficit solutions.
  • Interactive Voice Narrator: Friendly conversational voice guidance explaining app features and setup steps out loud in warm Arabic, ideal for new treasurers and effortless onboarding.
  • Multi-Month Instant WhatsApp Receipts: Record payments covering multiple past or upcoming months in a single tap; the app auto-calculates totals, words the amounts, and crafts a branded WhatsApp receipt instantly.
  • Smart Direct Push Notifications: Real-time alerts keeping residents and treasurers aligned on routine maintenance dates, general assembly assemblies, and monthly installment deadlines.
  • Ultra-Smooth Fluid Performance: Scroll position memory in units screen, instant offline loading, and optimized battery usage across all modern Android devices.

Version 1.0.205 October 2026

Corporate Banking-Grade Reports, Law 119/2008 Compliance & Zero-Trust Cloud Security

Financial reports completely elevated to global corporate banking aesthetics (Stripe/Ramp/Brex) matching Egyptian Building Law 119/2008 requirements.

  • Banking-Grade Financial Reports: Clean high-contrast monochrome layouts with running balance ledger, QR verification, and clearance seals.
  • Treasury Reconciliation Matrix: Detailed breakdown of cash on hand vs. InstaPay bank transfers with collection efficiency metrics.
  • Accounts Receivable Aging (AR Matrix): Structured aging buckets (<30 days, 31-60, 90+) in accordance with Article 80 of Egyptian Law 119/2008.
  • Special Project Arrears Inclusion: Monthly summary tables now explicitly account for pending project installments with distinct status badges.
  • Zero-Trust Cloud Security Hardening: Zero API keys bundled in binary; all AI calls routed through secure authenticated Cloud Functions proxy.
